Apartments in Gainesville on Campus Bus Routes

University of Florida students pay a transportation fee as part of their tuition that allows them to ride RTS (Regional Transit System) buses anywhere on or off campus for free. The RTS system has over 40 routes connecting Gainesville neighborhoods to the UF campus core.

The most student-friendly bus routes along the SW 34th Street and Archer Road corridors include:
- Route 1: Runs along University Avenue and connects the main campus entrance to downtown and west Gainesville.
- Route 9: Serves the SW 34th Street corridor and connects to the Reitz Union bus depot on campus.
- Route 12: Connects Archer Road apartment complexes directly to campus.
- Route 34: Archer Road express, one of the most heavily used routes by students living south of campus.
- Route 35: Serves SW 34th Street and Butler Plaza area.
- Route 38: Connects apartments along SW 34th Street to the Health Science Center.
- Route 75: Runs to Celebration Pointe and the SW Gainesville corridor.

The GNV RideRTS app provides real-time bus tracking so students know exactly when their bus is arriving. Most buses run every 15–30 minutes during peak hours (7 AM–6 PM on weekdays) and less frequently in the evenings and on weekends.

Apartments Closest to Bus Stops on SW 34th Street

Stoneridge Apartments (3800 SW 34th St) — Has a bus stop directly in front of the complex. Routes 9, 35 stop here. Peak frequency during fall and spring semesters is every 20 minutes.

Centric on 34th (SW 39th Blvd off 34th St) — Bus stop nearby on 34th Street. Routes 9 and 35 accessible within a short walk.

Greenwich Green (SW 39th Blvd) — On-site RTS bus stop. This is one of the selling points management highlights for prospective tenants. Routes 9 and 35 serve this stop.

The Quarters Gainesville (4000 SW 37th Blvd) — About a 10-minute bus ride to campus. Bus stop is within a 3-minute walk of the complex entrance. Route 35 provides the most direct connection.

Tips for Student Commuters

Sign up for RTS alerts through myRTS or the GNV RideRTS app to get notified of route changes, especially at the start of each semester when new schedules take effect. Fall and spring semester schedules differ significantly from summer schedules — always check before the semester starts.

Students living along Archer Road can typically reach the main campus bus depot (Reitz Union) in 10–20 minutes depending on traffic. Morning bus rides between 8 AM and 9:30 AM can be crowded; leaving 10 minutes earlier often means a seat.

Biking is a common alternative for students within 2 miles of campus. Gainesville has dedicated bike lanes along SW 13th Street, University Avenue, and parts of 34th Street. Many apartment complexes on 34th Street offer covered bike storage.
