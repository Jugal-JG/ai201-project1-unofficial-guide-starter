Phone: (352) 616-7167

Website: thequartersgainesville.com

About The Quarters

The Quarters Gainesville is a large student-oriented apartment community designed for groups of 3–4 roommates. The complex features modern, fully furnished units with a focus on student lifestyle amenities. It is one of the larger complexes in the SW 34th Street corridor, popular with UF undergraduates and international students.

Floor Plans and Pricing

3 Bedroom / 3 Bathroom (furnished):
- Square footage: approximately 1,200 sq ft
- Per-person rent: $650–$780/month (2025 rates, all-inclusive)

4 Bedroom / 4 Bathroom (furnished):
- Square footage: approximately 1,400 sq ft
- Per-person rent: $560–$680/month (2025 rates, all-inclusive)
- This is one of the most affordable per-person options in the Gainesville student market

All rates are typically all-inclusive (rent + utilities + internet). Upgraded/renovated units are available at a premium of approximately $50–$80/month per person.

Unit Features
- Fully furnished (bed, dresser, desk, sofa, dining table)
- Private bedroom with private bathroom in most floor plans
- In-unit or in-building washer/dryer access
- High-speed internet included
- Modern finishes in recently renovated units
- Air conditioning

Community Amenities
- One of the largest pool areas in Gainesville student housing, with a jumbo TV/outdoor screen
- Outdoor grilling stations
- 24-hour fitness center
- Resident lounge with private study labs and computer stations
- Fenced dog park (pet-friendly)
- Game room
- On-site management and maintenance

Transportation

Bus access: RTS Route 35 stops within a 3-minute walk of the complex entrance. The ride to UF's main campus (Reitz Union bus depot) takes approximately 10 minutes. During peak hours, buses run every 20–25 minutes.

Bike: The 34th Street bike lane is accessible from the complex. Students frequently bike to campus in approximately 15–20 minutes.

Car: Butler Plaza and Celebration Pointe are both within a 5-minute drive, providing access to Target, Whole Foods, Trader Joe's, and major restaurant chains.

Community Notes

The Quarters attracts a mix of UF undergraduates and a significant population of international students, particularly from South and East Asia. The large pool area is a social hub, especially on weekends. The study labs are well-regarded by residents who need quiet space outside of their apartments.

The Quarters participates in the UF off-campus housing portal and maintains a presence at UF housing fairs each spring. Lease signing for the following academic year typically opens in January–February, and popular floor plans (especially 4BR) fill up quickly.

Common resident feedback: The complex is large enough that community events can feel impersonal. However, individual buildings develop their own social dynamics. Management responsiveness is generally rated above average for the price point.
