UF off campus housing is abundant, affordable by Florida standards, and available in a range of styles from garden-style apartment complexes near campus to single-family homes in established residential neighborhoods. Whether you are a first-year student moving out of the dorms or a graduate student looking for a quieter home base, Gainesville's rental market has good options across the spectrum.

Gainesville Neighborhoods for UF Off Campus Housing
University Avenue is the main artery connecting Gainesville to the University of Florida campus, and the neighborhoods along and around it offer the highest concentration of UF off campus housing. Student apartment complexes cluster along SW Archer Road, 34th Street, and the streets immediately surrounding the main campus entrance on 13th Street.
Midtown Gainesville, roughly defined by the area between campus and the historic downtown, is popular among upper-division and graduate students who want a neighborhood feel with access to independent restaurants, coffee shops, and nightlife. The area features a mix of apartment buildings, duplexes, and houses, and is walkable to campus for students willing to make a fifteen to twenty minute walk.
East Gainesville offers more affordable housing options for students who prioritize space and value over proximity. Bus routes connect East Gainesville to the University of Florida campus, making it accessible for students without cars. Many houses in this area are rented by groups of three to five students and offer significantly more space per person than campus-adjacent apartments.
UF Off Campus Housing Costs in Gainesville
Gainesville's cost of living is notably favorable compared to Florida's coastal cities. Studio apartments near the University of Florida campus run $850 to $1,200 per month. One-bedroom units average $1,000 to $1,400. Shared two-bedroom apartments can bring individual costs to $600 to $900 per person, making roommate arrangements highly popular among undergraduates managing tuition and living costs simultaneously.
Utilities in Gainesville are generally not included in rent. Florida's humidity and heat mean air conditioning runs nearly year-round, adding $80 to $150 to monthly electricity costs. Many newer apartment complexes near campus include water and trash in the monthly rent, reducing utility management complexity for students new to independent living.
Gainesville's rental market has a clear seasonal rhythm driven by the UF academic calendar. Units near campus lease quickly in the February through May window as students plan for the following fall.

Getting Around Gainesville from UF Off Campus Housing
The Regional Transit System in Gainesville serves the University of Florida campus and surrounding neighborhoods with reliable, frequent service. Several routes run directly through the campus core and connect to the student apartment corridors along SW Archer Road and 34th Street. Most students living within three miles of campus can commute effectively by bus.
Cycling is very popular among UF students thanks to Gainesville's flat terrain and growing network of bike lanes. The city has invested significantly in cycling infrastructure connecting neighborhoods to campus, and many students prefer a bicycle commute for its speed, low cost, and health benefits during the academic year.
For students with cars, Gainesville's road network is manageable and parking near UF off campus housing is generally more available and less expensive than near most urban university campuses. Campus parking permits are competitive, but many students living off campus walk, bike, or take the bus rather than driving to class.
Lease Tips for UF Students in Gainesville
Florida landlord-tenant law requires landlords to return security deposits within 15 days if there are no deductions, or within 30 days with written itemization of any deductions. Document your apartment's condition with photographs and written communication to your landlord at both move-in and move-out to protect your deposit.
Read your lease carefully, particularly around early termination rights, guest policies, and maintenance responsibilities. Some off-campus student complexes near the University of Florida include mandatory fees for amenity packages—fitness centers, pools, study lounges—that add to your monthly cost beyond the base rent. Understand the total monthly commitment before signing.
Life Off Campus in Gainesville
Living in UF off campus housing gives you full access to one of Florida's most character-filled cities. Gainesville's independent restaurant and bar scene, its weekend farmers market, its proximity to natural springs like Ichetucknee and Silver Springs, and its deeply engaged local arts community make it a genuinely enjoyable place to live for students who invest in exploring beyond campus. The University of Florida's size ensures there is always something happening, and living off campus puts you closer to the city that hosts it all.
