Yelp Reviews — UF Apartments in Gainesville, FL (manually collected)

--- Lark Gainesville ---
Rating: 4.3 / 5

"Lark is one of the nicest apartment buildings near UF. The rooftop views are incredible, the amenities are premium, and the staff is always helpful. You pay for the luxury but it's worth it if budget isn't a constraint."

"Love living at Lark. The building is modern, my unit has floor-to-ceiling windows, and the study rooms are great for finals season. The coffee shop in the building is a bonus I use every day."

--- Sweetwater ---
Rating: 4.1 / 5

"Sweetwater on University Ave is one block from campus — you literally cannot get more convenient. The rooftop pool with a jumbotron is a fun perk. Prices are high but location offsets the cost."

"The coffee shop on the ground floor is a gem. Staff is friendly and organized. The only downside is that it gets very loud on football game days since it's so close to campus."

--- University Corners ---
Rating: 3.9 / 5

"Great location right on University Avenue. Mix of retail and residential. The noise from the street can be an issue but the walkability is unmatched. Perfect for students who don't own a car."

--- Trimark Properties (various complexes) ---
Rating: 3.7 / 5

"Trimark manages several older apartment buildings near campus. Prices are lower than the newer luxury complexes. Maintenance is decent but not fast. Good option if you want to be near UF without paying luxury prices."

"I've rented from Trimark for two years. The units are dated but functional. No frills but no major problems either. Management responds to emails within a day or two."

--- Stoneridge Apartments ---
Rating: 3.4 / 5

"Stoneridge has been around forever and it shows — the buildings are older but the community feel is great. Very popular with Indian and South Asian students. You'll find a lot of people to carpool with or share cooking responsibilities."

"Good location on SW 34th Street with easy bus access. The units are spacious for the price. Maintenance can be slow for non-urgent issues. The community is diverse and welcoming, especially for international students."

"The Indian community here is strong. There are always people to connect with for rides to Indian grocery stores like Patel Brothers or Apna Bazar. This social network is something you won't find at the luxury complexes."

"Price per bedroom is among the best on the 34th Street corridor. For a 3-bedroom unit we paid around $700/person in 2025. Older appliances but clean and well-kept common areas."

--- Centric on 34th ---
Rating: 3.6 / 5

"Centric is a solid option for grad students. 2 and 3 bedroom units, in-unit washer/dryer, and a quiet atmosphere that's more suitable for studying than the big party complexes. Close to Butler Plaza for grocery shopping."

"Management is professional and communication is clear. No major maintenance issues in my year there. The location requires a bus or bike to get to the main campus but the 34th Street RTS stop is right there."
