Centric on 34th Apartments — Gainesville, FL

Address: 3415 SW 39th Blvd, Gainesville, FL 32608
Phone: (352) 373-5932
Distance to UF: 2.4 miles (approximately 5 minutes by car, 15–20 minutes by bus)

About Centric on 34th

Centric on 34th offers spacious 2 and 3-bedroom apartment homes in a prime Gainesville location just minutes from the University of Florida, UF Health Shands Hospital, and Butler Plaza. Built in 2000, the community consists of 119 units across 3 stories.

The complex is popular with UF graduate students, medical and pharmacy students, and working professionals affiliated with UF Health due to its proximity to both the main campus and the Health Science Center.

Neighborhood and Transportation

Centric on 34th is located in the 34th Street corridor, within walking distance of several Indian and international grocery stores along Archer Road. Butler Plaza is 0.8 miles away and includes Whole Foods, Target, TJ Maxx, and dozens of restaurants.

RTS bus access: The 34th Street bus stop (Routes 9, 35) is approximately a 5-minute walk from the complex entrance. From this stop, the ride to the UF Reitz Union bus depot takes approximately 15–18 minutes.

Neighboring complexes: Stoneridge Apartments, Greenwich Green, and The Quarters Gainesville are all within 0.5 miles, making this part of the 34th Street corridor a connected community for UF students.

Resident Notes

Centric is generally quieter than the large luxury complexes closer to campus, making it a popular choice for graduate and professional students who prioritize studying over social amenities. The in-unit laundry is a feature many residents cite as a key reason for choosing Centric over complexes with shared laundry facilities. Management turnover has been relatively stable compared to some other Gainesville complexes.
