Greenwich Green Apartments — Gainesville, FL

Address: SW 39th Blvd, Gainesville, FL 32608 (near SW 34th Street)
Distance to UF: approximately 2 miles
Managed by: Apartments in Gainesville (apartmentsingainesville.com)

About Greenwich Green

Greenwich Green is a mid-size apartment community located in the SW 34th Street corridor, approximately 2 miles from the University of Florida campus. The complex is popular with UF graduate students and international students, particularly from South Asian backgrounds, due to its affordability, proximity to Indian grocery stores on Archer Road, and the presence of an on-site RTS bus stop.

Transportation

The on-site RTS bus stop is a major advantage of Greenwich Green. Students can board the bus directly from the property without walking to a separate stop. Routes 9 and 35 connect to UF's main campus in approximately 15–20 minutes. This is particularly valuable in Gainesville's summer heat.

Proximity to Indian Grocery and Services

Greenwich Green's location in the 34th Street corridor places it within a 5–10 minute drive of:
- Patel Brothers (Indian grocery) on Archer Road
- Apna Bazar (South Asian grocery) on Archer Road
- Multiple Indian restaurants along Archer Road and 34th Street

This proximity is frequently cited by residents from India and other South Asian countries as a significant quality-of-life factor.

Resident Notes

Greenwich Green attracts a quieter demographic than the large luxury complexes closer to campus. The community skews toward graduate students, young professionals, and international students who value affordability and outdoor amenity space (tennis, basketball) over party culture and premium interiors.

The complex is managed by Apartments in Gainesville, which manages several properties in the corridor. Maintenance response times are generally reported as acceptable for non-urgent requests (2–5 business days) and fast for emergencies. The fitness studio was renovated in 2023 and is frequently mentioned as a highlight.

Lease terms are typically 12 months starting in August, with some flexibility for spring starts. Utilities (water, trash, sewer) are typically included; electricity and internet are tenant responsibility unless otherwise negotiated.
