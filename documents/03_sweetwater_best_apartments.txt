9 Best Apartments for Students at The University of Florida
1. The Standard
The Standard, located on W University and 13th St, opened in Fall 2017. Photo courtesy of CHW.
Address:
1360 W University Ave, Gainesville, FL, 32603
Opened in:
Fall 2017
The Standard is an off-campus apartment complex offering student housing a three-minute walk away from UF. Community amenities include a rooftop pool, fitness center, study lounge spaces, and a clubhouse. Floor plans range from studios to six bedrooms, with penthouse layouts that span two floors. Parking options are also available for cars and scooters.
2. Theory
Address:
1210 NW 5th Ave, Gainesville FL 32601
Opened in:
Fall 2023
Theory Gainesville offers student housing located only minutes away from UF campus, near Ben Hill Griffin Stadium. Community amenities include a rooftop lounge and pool, 24/7 rooftop gym, study rooms and lounge. Floor plans range from studio to five-bedroom layouts.
3. Hub on 3rd
Hub on 3rd, located on NW 3rd and 13th St, opened in Fall 2020. Photo courtesy of Hub on 3rd.
Address:
1258 NW 3rd Ave, Gainesville, FL 32601
Opened in:
Fall 2020
Hub on 3rd offers conveniently located student apartments for UF student residents, about a six-minute walk to campus. Community amenities include a rooftop pool with a hot tub, fitness center with a yoga studio, and a study lounge. Floor plans range from studios to five bedrooms and townhouse layouts. Parking for scooters and cars is available on a first-come, first-served basis.

5. The Griffin
Address:
1205 SW 11th Ave, Gainesville, FL 32601

6. Legacy at The Standard

They offer studio, 1×1, 2×2, 3×3, 4×2, 4×4, 5×3, 5×5, 5×5.5, and 6×4 floor plans.
7. Ufora
Address:
1218 SW 5th Ave, Gainesville, FL 32601

Floor plans include studios to 4x4s, as well as 6×6 townhomes and 8×8 microstudios.
