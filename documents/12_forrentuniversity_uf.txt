Overview of Off-Campus Housing Near UF

The University of Florida campus sits at the center of Gainesville with a large concentration of student-oriented apartment complexes within a 3-mile radius. Students have a wide range of options from luxury high-rises on University Avenue to garden-style complexes along the SW 34th Street corridor.

Helpful Tips for UF Off-Campus Renters

Lease timing: The best apartments near UF lease out February through May for the following fall semester. Waiting until summer often means limited options and higher prices on remaining units.

Lease length: Most student complexes offer 12-month leases starting in August. Some offer shorter 9-month academic-year leases at a premium. A few allow month-to-month after the initial term.

Utilities: Many student apartments advertise "all bills paid" or "utilities included." Confirm exactly what is covered — typically electricity has a cap (e.g., $50–$75/month) beyond which you pay the overage. Internet is usually fully included.

Guarantor requirements: Most complexes require a guarantor (parent or guardian) for students without sufficient income. Some allow a security deposit in lieu of a guarantor.

Subletting: Check your lease carefully. Many Gainesville student apartments prohibit subletting or charge a fee for lease transfers. This matters if you study abroad or leave for a summer internship.
